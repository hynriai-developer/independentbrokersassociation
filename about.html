About the association

We exist to strengthen independent real estate brokerages.

Our mission

To support, educate and represent independent real estate brokers so they can serve clients with integrity and compete confidently in their markets.

Our values

  • Integrity. Clients first, ethics always.
  • Independence. Brokers decide how they run their business.
  • Collaboration. We grow faster when we share what works.
  • Professionalism. Higher standards lift the whole industry.

Who we serve

Licensed real estate brokers and brokerage owners who operate independently, from solo brokers to growing boutique firms.
