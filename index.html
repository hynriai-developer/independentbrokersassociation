Independence, with a community behind it.

The Independent Brokers Association connects independent real estate brokers with education, advocacy, referrals and peer support, so you can run your own brokerage without running it alone.

Become a member Learn more

Why brokers join

Advocacy

A collective voice on the issues that affect independent brokerages, from regulation to market practices.

Education

Practical training on compliance, operations, negotiation and growing a brokerage on your own terms.

Network

Referrals, co-brokering and mentorship with fellow independents you can trust.

Resources

Templates, checklists and vendor guides to save time and reduce risk.

Events

Local meetups, webinars and an annual gathering for brokers and their teams.

Credibility

Show clients and agents you are part of a professional community with shared standards.

Built by brokers, for brokers

Independent brokers carry the full weight of their business: compliance, trust accounts, recruiting, marketing and technology. The association exists to make that load lighter through shared knowledge and collective strength.
